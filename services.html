Frequently Asked Questions

Common queries regarding virtual sessions, dietary recommendations, and scheduling.

help_outline How does an Online Ayurveda Consultation work effectively without physical pulse check? expand_more
Classical Ayurveda incorporates comprehensive diagnostic modes: Prashna Pariksha (thorough clinical interrogation), Darshana (visual observation of tongue, eyes, facial symmetry, skin quality), and health chronology analysis. In virtual settings, Dr. Aditya conducts structured questioning alongside visual reviews to evaluate constitutional Dosha balance and metabolic Agni with high clinical precision.
help_outline Can I combine Ayurvedic guidance with my current allopathic medicines? expand_more
Yes, seamlessly. Dr. Aditya evaluates current medications, diagnostic lab reports, and ongoing therapies. Ayurvedic lifestyle, diet, and herbal protocols are designed to complement ongoing medical care without drug-herb contraindications. We never ask you to discontinue essential medications without your prescribing doctor's consent.
help_outline Are the dietary plans rigid or difficult to follow with a busy job? expand_more
Not at all. Classical Ahara principles prioritize realistic, sustainable adaptations tailored to your household and work commitments. Rather than imposing impractical restrictions, we focus on meal timing, digestive spices (Deepana/Pachana), and eliminating conflicting combinations (Viruddha Ahara) using everyday food items.
help_outline How soon will I receive my written health protocol after the session? expand_more
Following your consultation, Dr. Aditya formulates your comprehensive protocol. A customized PDF document detailing lifestyle routines, diet charts, and herbal suggestions is delivered via WhatsApp and email within 24 hours of your appointment.
